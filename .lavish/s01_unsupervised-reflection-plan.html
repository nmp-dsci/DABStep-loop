2 · the recipe being imitated

2 · NVIDIA — three phases; reflection writes the prompt, consistency picks the method

  • Result: Haiku 4.5 at inference, 89.95% hard against 66.93% for Claude Code with Opus 4.5; 20 s per task against 10 min.
  • Learning phase (heavy model, full tools): builds helper.py, a markdown manual of domain rules, and a few-shot set from the dev split.

Judge per question, or clusters over all questions? Both, in that order, and neither is embeddings

  • Clustering first, not a model: blank the variable parts → 106 templates → 12 operations by an ordered regex table, before any run.
  • Same family = same computation, not similar wording.
  • Then one judge per family: the offline session reads the family's probe traces side by side and rules on the method with the manual open.
  • Why not per question: a per-question judge cannot see that two siblings were solved two ways, and with no gold has nothing to judge against.

Why regex over semantic embeddings for these 450

  • Permutations, not paraphrases: the paper says 95 core questions; a blanking rule states exactly what an embedding would only approximate.
  • Auditable: a card can cite the regex that put a task in F03; it cannot cite a centroid.
  • Wording misleads: "total fees paid in March" and "delta if fee 384's rate changed" share words and no computation.
  • Embeddings as a check: embed the 450 locally, k-means at k = 12, report the confusion against the regex families. A split family or a merged pair gets a second look (F07/F08, §10). It never decides.

3b · three lenses on the 450

3b · Three lenses — regex, embeddings and a Sonnet membership pass must agree, or the task is a boundary case

  • Lens 1 · regex families (§3): deterministic, auditable, the id every card carries.
  • Lens 2 · embedding clusters: a local sentence model embeds the 450 questions once; k-means with k chosen by silhouette over 8–16 (expected near 12); no API call.
  • Lens 3 · Sonnet membership, one pass over the 450: an agent first writes a one-paragraph summary of each embedding cluster from its questions; Sonnet then reads every question with the summaries and returns [{cluster, confidence}], several entries allowed, so a question may belong to two clusters.
  • Agreement: adjusted Rand index and a confusion matrix, lens against lens; a question whose three lenses disagree, or whose lens-3 membership is split, is a boundary task.
  • What boundary tasks are for: the sampler (§5, step 1) draws them first. They are where the method is likeliest to flip, so they are the cheapest place to find an inconsistency.

Questions or traces? Both, in this order

  • Cluster the questions first (lenses 1–3): no run needed, defines the families and the sampling frame, and is the same for every champion.
  • Then cluster the traces (lens 4): the champion's probe traces grouped by method signature (helper calls, pandas operations, turns). This is per champion and is what changes cycle to cycle.
  • The comparison is the measurement: one question cluster answered by two method clusters is exactly NVIDIA's "conflicting methods" flag. Per family, the share on the modal method is S1.
  • Confidence per question = lens-3 membership confidence × the family's signals (S1, S2, S3, S5). Low confidence, high count is where the system prompt gets its next rule.
  • What clustering questions alone cannot do: say how well the agent answered them. Only the trace lens can, so the prompt is improved from lens 4 with lenses 1–3 as the frame.

Cost and what gets committed

  • Embeddings: one local run over 450 short strings; an optional dependency (sentence-transformers), no key.
  • Cluster summaries: one Sonnet call per cluster (≈ 12), a paragraph each.
  • Membership pass: the 450 in batches of 30 with the summaries in context, ≈ 15 calls, ≈ 0.1M tokens in, 30k out.
  • Committed: loop/families/lenses.json (per task: L1 id, L2 cluster, L3 memberships with confidence, boundary flag) and summaries.json; shown on the Families page as a confusion matrix and a boundary list.
  • Rule kept: none of this reads gold or task_scores; a summary describes questions, never answers.

4 · signals without gold

4 · Signals — five things a run tells you with no answer key

  • No signal reads a gold answer or the leaderboard's task_scores.
  • Computed per probe task from its trace and its siblings' answers, then aggregated per family.
  • Two questions answered: is the method stable across the family (S1, S2); is the method right (S3, S4).
  • Consistency alone is not enough: the invariants and the audit stop a family converging on a confident wrong method.

Why invariants, concretely

  • F04: 1871 asks January's delta if fee 384's rate became 1; a sibling asks 2023's. Year = Σ months; a third sibling with the rate unchanged = 0. Three answers, no gold, and a contradiction is a bug with a trace attached.
  • F09 ↔ F03: the per-scheme totals that pick the "steer to" scheme must sum to the month's total fees. If not, one family is wrong and the reflector says which.

What consistency cannot tell you

  • All 25 F10 probes can agree and all be wrong: 2697 has failed three cycles with a stable method; S1 and S2 would score it 1.0.
  • So verified needs S3 passed and an S4 audit that cites the manual section the method follows.
  • Anchored families re-check their dev task every cycle.
  • The audit is the expensive step: once per cycle, offline, over the sampled traces, as NVIDIA describes.

5 · the loop

5 · Loop — sample, probe, signal, card, optimise, guard; one ledger line

  • make uloop wraps the existing cycle.
  • Steps 1 to 4 are new and run before the optimiser: sample, probe, signals, cards.
  • Step 5 is the current optimiser with one extra prompt block.
  • Step 6 pairs the evaluation: dev-10 scored as now, plus the same probe sample re-run so every gold-free signal is a before/after pair, like the McNemar pairs.
  1. Sample. loop/sampler.py draws k tasks per family from the 450 with a fixed seed, boundary tasks (§3b) first, never a dev-10 id, and adds the siblings each invariant needs (a day, its month, its year for F01 and F03). Cards with status verified get k = 1 to spot regressions; open and provisional get the full k.
  2. Probe. dabstep probe --agent v2 --k 3 --passes 2 runs the sample through the unchanged runner into runs/<ts>_v2_probe_haiku/ with correct: null on every row. No submission file is written; the eval --split all confirmation stays as it is.
  3. Signals. loop/signals.py computes S1, S2, S3, S5 from the run folder alone and writes signals.json next to it, one block per family.
  4. Cards. dabstep ureflect --run <probe> is the current reflection session with a new prompt: per family, the traces, the signals, the current card, and the instruction to choose the canonical method with a manual citation, name the helper entry point, pick one few-shot example from a probe that passed every check, and set the status. It writes loop/families/Fnn.json and one ledger line of kind ureflect.
  5. Optimise. The existing optimiser prompt gains a block "Question families" that renders every card, ordered open first, plus the S3 contradictions with their traces. Its instructions add one rule: system.md gets a routing table (family → helper entry point → format) and at most one few-shot per family; helper.py gets one entry point per family that lacks one.
  6. Paired evaluation. The challenger runs dev-10 (scored, as now) and the same probe sample (unscored, same seed). Signals are recomputed so each family has a before and after.
  7. Gate. §7; the verdict and both signal blocks go into the ledger entry (kind ucycle), and the registry promotes or holds as today.

7 · promotion without gold

7 · Gate — dev-10 must not break; the gold-free signals must move

  • McNemar stays for what it is good at: refusing a challenger that breaks a gold task.
  • It cannot be the whole gate: dev-10 has one failure left, so p ≥ 0.5 for any challenger.
  • Three options below; A is recommended and is what fig 2 draws.

A · No dev break, and the paired signals improverecommended

Promote when broken = 0 on dev-10 and the composite over the probe sample rises: S3 invariants passed strictly up, or S1 method consistency up with S3 not down, with S5 format at 1.0. Fixed dev tasks still count; the McNemar p is recorded, not required.

Cost: a challenger can be promoted on evidence with no gold in it. That is the point, and the risk in §10 is the counterweight: a card only reaches verified through S3 and the audit.

B · Keep the McNemar gate alone

Promote when p < 0.05 on dev-10, as today. Cards and signals are recorded for the reader but never decide.

Cost: with one dev failure left, no challenger can ever be promoted again (best case p = 0.5), so every unsupervised cycle ends in hold and the champion never learns a family. Honest, and useless.

C · A, plus a human reads the cards before promotion

Promote when A holds and you approve the changed cards in the Families page (a queued decision, like this artifact's forms).

Cost: the loop is no longer unattended; each cycle waits for a review. Reasonable for the first two cycles, then switch to A.

10 · risks & open questions

10 · Risks — consistency is not correctness, and the plan knows it

Failure modes and the mechanism for each

  • A family converges on a stable wrong method. S1 and S2 would read 1.0. Only S3 and the audit can set verified; anchored families re-check their dev task every cycle; F10 stays open until 2697 passes.
  • The routing table becomes a hidden answer key. The optimiser rule forbids task-specific branches, the guard already rejects hard-coded ids, and the CI gate greps cards and prompts for any of the 450 task ids or literal answers.
  • Leakage from the leaderboard. Unchanged: no code path reads task_scores; the probe runner has no scorer; the rule in CLAUDE.md stands.
  • "Runs on the 450" reads like "scores the 450". A probe run has correct: null on every row and writes no submission; the viewer labels it unscored; M8 remains the first scoring.
  • Few-shot lines bloat the prompt and the tokens KPI. One line per family, measured on dev-10 every cycle; the tokens-per-question KPI is in the ledger entry.
  • An invariant is wrong, not the agent. Every invariant is a pure function with a unit test in both directions, and a failed invariant is shown to the reflector as a claim to check, not a verdict.
  • Sampling misses the template that breaks. Seeds rotate per cycle while status is provisional; a card records which templates it has seen.
